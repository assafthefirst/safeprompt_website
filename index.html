How SafePrompt keeps personal data out of AI chats

SafePrompt sits on the chat page you already use. You keep typing the way you do now.

  1. 1

    It warns you before you send

    Press Enter or Send as usual. If SafePrompt spots sensitive data, it lists what it found and lets you choose: Secure & send, Send anyway, or go back and edit.

    Sensitive data detected×

    This message contains sensitive data. Secure & send replaces it with placeholders before sending.

    NameEmailPhone numberID
    Send anywaySecure & send
    Write a follow-up email to Jane Cohen (jane.cohen@acme.com, 054-381-2297) about invoice #4471 for client ID 3049-7731.
  2. 2

    Your data becomes placeholders

    One click and each detail is swapped for a token like [[SP_EMAIL_1]]. The AI gets the shape of your request, never the real values. Clicking twice won't double-mask.

    Secured!
    Write a follow-up email to [[SP_NAME_1]] ([[SP_EMAIL_1]], [[SP_PHONE_1]]) about invoice #4471 for client ID [[SP_ID_1]].
  3. 3

    Mask anything, just highlight it

    A nickname, a case name, a project codename. Select it and click Mask as PII. Every match in that prompt is replaced, including things auto-detect wouldn't know about.

    Mask as PII Summarize the Rosenberg case notes and draft next steps for the Rosenberg family.
  4. 4

    Reveal the real details, on your screen

    When the answer arrives, click Reveal Original Data. Placeholders turn back into the real names and numbers right on the page, so you can read and copy a finished draft.

    Subject: Invoice #4471, quick follow-up

    Hi Jane Cohen,

    Just following up on invoice #4471 for client 3049-7731. If it's easier, I can call you at 054-381-2297 this week to wrap it up.

ChatGPT privacy questions, answered

Is it safe to share personal information with ChatGPT?

It's safest not to. On consumer plans, OpenAI says it may use your conversations to train its models unless you turn off "Improve the model for everyone" in Settings, Data controls. Even with training off, your chats are still kept in your account. SafePrompt replaces names, emails, phone numbers, IDs and card numbers with placeholders before you send, so the real values never reach ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

What should you never share with ChatGPT or other AI chatbots?

Anything that identifies a real person or account: full names with contact details, ID and passport numbers, credit card and bank details, home addresses, and other people's data from work, like client records, payroll lines or patient notes. SafePrompt warns you when a prompt contains these and masks them in one click.

How do I anonymize a prompt before sending it to an AI?

Install SafePrompt and type as usual. When you press Send, it lists the personal data it found and offers Secure & send, which swaps each detail for a placeholder like [[SP_EMAIL_1]]. You can also highlight any word and click Mask as PII. When the answer comes back, Reveal Original Data puts the real values back on your screen.

Can SafePrompt see my prompts?

No. Detection and masking run inside the extension in your browser. Your prompts and the AI's answers aren't sent to SafePrompt, and nothing is stored on a server.

Isn't it enough to just be careful?

Most leaks aren't decisions, they're habits: pasting a whole email thread, a contract or a log file without reading every line. SafePrompt checks at the moment that matters, when you press Send, and only interrupts when it finds something.

Will the AI still understand what I'm asking?

Yes. Placeholders keep the structure of your request, and SafePrompt adds a short line asking the model to keep the tokens unchanged. When the answer comes back, Reveal Original Data puts the real values in place.

Which AI sites does it work on?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft Copilot and DeepSeek, in Chrome.

Can I send something without masking it?

Yes. Choose Send anyway in the warning. It applies to that one prompt, and you'll be warned again next time. You can also pause protection from the extension popup.

What happens when I refresh or close the tab?

The token map is kept per tab for about 30 minutes so Reveal still works after a refresh. Closing the browser, or clicking Clear Session History, wipes it.

What's included for free?

Warnings, detection and Mask as PII are always free. You also get 10 Secure Sends, with Reveal included. After that, Pro unlocks unlimited use for $6.99 a month, or $56.95 a year.

Something wasn't detected, or was flagged by mistake. What do I do?

Highlight anything that was missed and click Mask as PII. To help us improve detection, use Report an issue in the extension or email safeprompt1@gmail.com.
