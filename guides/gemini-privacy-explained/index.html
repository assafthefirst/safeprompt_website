Gemini privacy explained

Updated October 29, 2026, 6-minute read

Gemini is woven into Google's products: the Gemini app, Gmail, Docs and Android. That makes it convenient, and it also means your conversations sit inside the same Google account as your email and files. Here's what Google keeps, what you can change, and what no setting changes.

The main setting: Keep Activity

Gemini's main privacy control is called Keep Activity (it used to be called Gemini Apps Activity).

When it's on:

  • Your conversations are saved to your Google account.
  • They can be used to train and improve Google's AI models.
  • A sample of your future uploads may also be used to improve Google services.

When it's off:

  • New conversations aren't saved to your activity and aren't used for training, unless you send feedback.
  • Google still keeps each conversation for 72 hours, tied to your account, to respond to you and keep the service safe.

To turn it off: open Gemini, go to Settings & help, then Activity, and switch Keep Activity off.

Human review

Google says a subset of Gemini conversations is reviewed by people, including trained service providers, to improve its products. Before review, conversations are disconnected from your account. But reviewed conversations are kept for up to three years, and deleting your activity doesn't remove them.

Google's own advice is not to enter information you wouldn't want a reviewer to see, or Google to use to improve its services. That's the most important line in its privacy documentation.

Temporary Chat

Temporary Chat, added in August 2025, is Gemini's equivalent of a private window:

  • These chats don't appear in your history or activity.
  • They aren't used to personalize Gemini or train Google's models.
  • Google still keeps them for up to 72 hours to respond and handle any feedback you give.

Use it for one-off questions where you don't want a record in your account.

Personal context: Gemini remembers you

Since August 2025, Gemini can learn from your past conversations and remember details and preferences. This is controlled by a Your past chats with Gemini setting on the Personal context page, which is on by default where available. If you'd rather Gemini didn't build a picture of you from earlier chats, turn it off there.

A separate setting controls whether audio, video and screens you share through the microphone button or Gemini Live are used to improve Google services. It's off by default.

Gemini at work (Google Workspace)

If you use Gemini through a work or school Google Workspace account, different terms apply. Your organization's administrator controls the settings, and business data is generally not used to train models. Check your company's AI policy before using any AI tool with work material.

What no setting changes

Even with Keep Activity off and Temporary Chat on:

  • What you type is still sent to Google and kept for at least 72 hours.
  • Reviewed conversations can be kept for up to three years.
  • It's all in the same account as your email and files, so account security matters.

The only way to be sure a detail isn't stored is not to send it. Replace names, numbers and client details with placeholders before you press Send (how to anonymize a prompt), and keep the real ones on your side.

Quick checklist

  • Keep Activity off, unless you want your chats saved and used for training
  • Temporary Chat for anything sensitive
  • "Your past chats with Gemini" off, if you don't want Gemini to remember you
  • No IDs, card numbers, health details or other people's data in prompts
  • At work: check your organization's rules first
