Claude privacy explained

Updated October 29, 2026, 6-minute read

Claude has a reputation as a privacy-conscious AI, and Anthropic publishes clear rules about what it keeps. But those rules changed in late 2025, and the default is no longer "your chats are never used for training". Here's what applies today to the consumer plans (Free, Pro and Max), and what to change.

The main setting: Help improve Claude

Since late 2025, Anthropic can use chats and coding sessions from consumer accounts to train its models, if the Help improve Claude setting is on.

To check or change it:

  1. Click your name, then Settings.
  2. Open Privacy.
  3. Turn Help improve Claude off if you don't want your chats used for training.

What the setting does:

  • On: new and resumed chats can be used for training, and Anthropic may keep them, de-identified, for up to five years in its training pipeline.
  • Off: your previous and new chats won't be used to train future models. Data already used in training runs that were in progress, or in models already trained, can't be removed from them.

Business, government and education accounts and the API are covered by separate terms and aren't affected by this setting.

How long Claude keeps your chats

According to Anthropic's privacy center:

Situation How long it's kept
Chat you delete Removed from your history immediately, deleted from back-end systems within 30 days
Training on (Help improve Claude) De-identified copies up to 5 years in the training pipeline
Feedback you give (thumbs up/down, bug reports) 5 years
Chat flagged for breaking the usage policy Inputs and outputs up to 2 years; safety scores up to 7 years

Anthropic can also keep data longer when the law requires it or to resolve disputes.

Incognito chats

Claude's incognito mode is the closest thing to a private conversation. Anthropic says incognito chats are never used to improve its models, and they don't appear in your chat history.

Feedback is a hidden exception

Clicking thumbs up or down sends that conversation to Anthropic as feedback, and feedback data is kept for five years. If a chat contains anything sensitive, skip the feedback buttons.

What no setting changes

Even with training off and incognito on:

  • Your prompt is still sent to Anthropic and processed on its servers.
  • Deleted chats can take up to 30 days to disappear from its systems.
  • Safety review still applies if a conversation is flagged.

So the practical rule is the same as for any AI tool: don't send what you can't take back. Replace names, numbers and client details with placeholders (here's how), and keep the real values on your side.

Quick checklist

  • Help improve Claude set the way you want it (Settings, then Privacy)
  • Incognito for anything sensitive
  • No feedback clicks on conversations with personal data
  • Old chats you don't need deleted
  • No IDs, card numbers, health details or client data in prompts
